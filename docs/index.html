How it works

Home

Shows the selected month split into still to set aside, unpaid, paid, and saving ahead for future annual costs. Swipe to change month, from last year to next.

Calendar

One card per month, colored against the year's average, with a read-only breakdown on tap. Swipe to change year, within the range your data covers.

Settings

Base currency, decimals, tracked locations, exchange rates, and iCloud controls — all in one place.

Your data stays yours

All expense data is stored locally on the device using SwiftData — no account and no network connection required. iCloud sync is off by default and, when enabled, stays inside your own private iCloud account. There is no analytics and no remote backend beyond your own iCloud.
